Contract Renewal Hub: in-depth test script

Every part of the Contract Renewal Hub: the renewal urgency dashboard, Copy Forward and contract dates, renewal lines and price changes, the renewal document, the QC-to-go-live workflow, the anomaly check, admin settings, and what the app writes to the Opportunities and Quotes & Invoices boards.

Prepared by Mutherboard · November 2026 · Version 1 · 55 tests · 4–5 hours, plus a short check the next morning · For: Account managers and CS managers who handle renewals, a colleague acting as QC reviewer, a renewal admin, and the people set up as Contracts Manager and Finance recipients

Before you start

The Contract Renewal Hub opens on an item on the Opportunities board. On a won contract it creates the renewal in one click (Copy Forward), copying every active quote line with new dates and a new Quote No. On the renewal it then takes you through repricing, the renewal document, quality check, customer acceptance, the anomaly check and finally making the new contract live.

What you need

  • Access to the Opportunities, Quotes & Invoices, Companies, People, Sites and Products & Services boards, and the Contract Renewal Hub view on Opportunities items (open an item and pick the Contract Renewal Hub view; ask your admin if you can’t see it).
  • A renewal admin. Until anyone is listed as a Renewal admin, only the owners of the Opportunities board can change the app’s settings, so the first admin test (B1) must be done by a board owner.
  • A second person who is not a renewal admin (for B2) and a colleague to act as QC reviewer (K3–K5). The reviewer must be subscribed to the Opportunities board, because the reviewer list is built from the board’s subscribers.
  • Before section F: B1 done (renewal admins, Contracts Manager, Finance recipients, confirmation email template), B3 checked (starting quote number) and B4 done (a Legionella price increase from 1 Jan 2027).
  • A small PDF on your computer called “TEST – Sam – PO.pdf” (for K7).
  • When a step says reload, close the item and open it again (or refresh the browser). The app does not notice changes you make directly on a board until you reload.
  • Most actions show a short pop-up message at the edge of the screen; the expected wording is given in bold. If an action clearly worked but no pop-up appeared, note it once in your feedback rather than failing every test.
  • Some renewal columns on the Opportunities board start with an orange dot (for example 🟠QC Status). This script leaves the dot out of column names.
  • Nothing in this app emails a client. Even so, use your own email address wherever an email address is asked for.

Recording your results

Under each test, choose Pass, Fail or Blocked (you could not run it), and add a note for anything that is not a pass: what you did, what you expected and what you saw. A link to the item or a screenshot helps us fix things quickly.

Your marks are saved in this browser only, separately for each app’s script. When you finish, go to Send your results, copy them and email them to the Mutherboard project team.

Tests marked “Overnight” depend on a date check that runs each morning. Set things up as the test describes and check the result the next day.

Help and FAQs

Why can’t I see the Copy Forward button?
The amber panel under the header says why: Deal Stage is not Won, nothing is Contract or Repeat, or the contract dates are missing or under a year. “Renewal created” with Open renewal means a renewal already exists. If Copy Forward says “Link the Account on this opportunity first”, the company was only found through the contact – use Save to this item (D3).
Every renewal says “Copy Forward isn’t available yet – Deal Stage is blank”. Is that wrong?
No. A renewal’s Deal Stage is only set to Won by Make Contract Live, so the panel shows until then.
Copy Forward only copied some lines. What now?
Stay on that screen and click Retry failed lines (F7). The button is only offered on the screen where Copy Forward ran. If you have already left it, tell the project team – running Copy Forward again just opens the existing renewal.
I added or removed a line but New Contract Value didn’t change.
New Contract Value is recalculated when a price change is applied or undone, and when the contract dates on a renewal are saved. The Contract value column on the Quotes & Invoices tab is always up to date.
The Price Change tab says “Failed to apply price change”.
The most common cause is that the Price Change Scope column on the Opportunities board has no label matching the work category you chose (or “All Services”). Ask an admin to add the label, then try again.
The anomaly check says “Failed to run anomaly check”.
Check that the renewal has its own company link (not just through the contact) and valid contract dates, then try again.
Who can change settings or override a stage?
Renewal admins listed on the Settings page. Until any are listed, Opportunities board owners can change settings and price rates – but only renewal admins can override a stage.
Where does the QC reviewer list come from?
From the people subscribed to the Opportunities board. If someone is missing, subscribe them to the board.
Does the app send anything to clients?
No. Mark proposal sent only records what you did, the renewal document downloads to your computer, and the confirmation email is saved as a draft update for someone to send.
An old contract has disappeared from the dashboard.
It is either One-off or superseded (a later Contract/Repeat opportunity on the same company, or a renewal that is Live or Won). Click Superseded (N) to see superseded contracts.
How is the Quote No chosen?
The higher of the starting quote number (Price increases page) and the highest Quote No on the Quotes & Invoices board, plus 1. Every line copied for one renewal gets the same number, and a retry keeps it. Lines added with Add Line are not given a Quote No.
A red box says some lines are “only linked one way”.
The line is in the opportunity’s 🔗Related Quotes but doesn’t link back through 🔗Opportunity, or the other way round – usually migrated data. The lines are still included; fix the link in monday so both sides agree.
I changed something on the board but the app still shows the old value.
Reload the item. The app only reads the board when it opens or after its own actions.
Can Make Contract Live be undone?
There is no undo button. A renewal admin can override the Renewal Stage on both items and change the Deal Stage on the board – ask the project team first.
How do I clean up afterwards?
Delete or archive the TEST items you created on Opportunities, Quotes & Invoices, Companies, People and Sites; delete the TEST price rates; check the starting quote number is back to its agreed value; and remove any TEST text from the confirmation email template.
