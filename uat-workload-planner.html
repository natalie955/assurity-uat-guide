Workload Planner: in-depth test script

Every screen of the Workload Planner: roles, the Unassigned jobs list, Find consultant matching and scores, allocating, re-allocating and returning visits, the map, workload and availability views, history, the dashboard and the Admin area.

Prepared by Mutherboard · November 2026 · Version 1 · 55 tests · 7–9 hours, best split over three sessions · For: Allocation admins and allocators, with a consultant and a second tester for the two-person steps

Before you start

The Workload Planner (the name at the top of the app reads Workload Map) is where the allocation team gives each visit a consultant. It lists every visit at Ready for Allocation, suggests the best-matched consultants with a score and plain-English reasons, checks for clashes, then writes the consultant and dates back to the Visits board and sends the consultant a monday.com notification. It also has a map, consultant workload and availability views, allocation history, a dashboard and an Admin area for consultants, competencies, targets, leave, work types and settings.

What you need

  • A monday.com login in the Assurity Consulting workspace that can open the Workload Planner and edit the Visits, Sites, Companies, Consultant Skills & Availability, Samples by Day, Lab Capacity and Report SLA Rules boards.
  • An allocation admin for tests marked Admin. Admins are the people listed in Admin → Settings → Allocation admins. While that list is empty, the owners of the Consultant Skills & Availability board are the admins.
  • Everyone who allocates must be listed under Allocators (or be an admin). Anyone else sees the app read-only. The app checks your access when it opens, so reload the app after your access changes.
  • A second tester for steps marked Two people. Their login is linked to the TEST support consultant and receives notifications. Each TEST consultant needs a login that isn’t already linked to another consultant row.
  • Tests that change Settings or a work type rule affect everyone straight away. Agree a time with the allocation team, write down the original value before you change it, and put it back as soon as the test is done.
  • Never allocate a real visit. Never allocate a TEST visit to a real consultant – they would get a monday.com notification. Putting your TEST company in the TEST consultants’ Approved accounts (test B1) keeps real consultants out of the suggestions for your TEST visits.

Recording your results

Under each test, choose Pass, Fail or Blocked (you could not run it), and add a note for anything that is not a pass: what you did, what you expected and what you saw. A link to the item or a screenshot helps us fix things quickly.

Your marks are saved in this browser only, separately for each app’s script. When you finish, go to Send your results, copy them and email them to the Mutherboard project team.

Tests marked “Overnight” depend on a date check that runs each morning. Set things up as the test describes and check the result the next day.

Allocation admin, with a second tester

Help and FAQs

Why does the app say “Workload Map” at the top?
That is the Workload Planner’s on-screen name. They are the same app.
I’ve just been made an allocator but I still see the read-only message.
The app checks your access when it opens. Reload the app (or close and reopen it in monday.com).
A yellow bar says “We couldn’t check your access, so the app is read-only for now.”
The access check failed, usually because of the connection. Click Try again. Until it works you can only view.
Why isn’t a consultant suggested?
Open Not suggested at the bottom of Find consultant. Each person has one reason: Active, Login, Account restriction, New customer, Competency, Fire level, First year or Availability.
Why does every real consultant say “Account restriction” on my TEST visits?
Your TEST company is in the TEST consultants’ Approved accounts. When any consultant has an account there, only those consultants can be allocated to it. This keeps real consultants out of your tests.
Why does a half-day visit show “1” under Days in Find consultant?
Days are rounded up to whole days when looking for free days. Planned Days at Site on the board stays as 0.5 unless you change the number of days.
The lead-time flag is red although the visit is exactly at the minimum lead time.
The app counts from now to the Monday of the proposed week. Part-way through a week, a visit exactly 4 weeks ahead is slightly under 4 weeks away.
The green box says “Couldn’t send … The allocation is saved …”.
The consultant, dates and stage were saved, but an update or notification didn’t go. It is recorded in the history. Tell the consultant directly.
Can I undo an allocation?
There is no undo. Use Return to queue (back to Ready for Allocation) or Re-allocate (needs a reason).
Does Change dates count as a re-allocation?
No. It keeps the same consultant, doesn’t change 🟣Times Re-allocated, and counts as an allocation in the history. It does reset Last Allocated to today and re-sends the “You've been allocated…” notification.
Where do bank holidays come from?
From gov.uk, refreshed daily. England & Wales holidays and Settings closures block everyone; Scottish holidays block Scottish sites and Scotland-based consultants; Northern Ireland holidays only when switched on in Settings.
The map background is blank.
You’ll see “The background map couldn’t load. Markers are still accurate.” The markers are still in the right places.
A banner says “Some bookings may be missing…”.
There were too many bookings to read in one go, so free-day counts may be overstated. Narrow the view (team, month) and tell the project team.
Who gets a notification when I allocate?
The lead and any supporting consultant, through their linked monday.com login. That is why TEST visits must only go to TEST consultants.
I changed a setting and everyone was affected.
Settings are shared. Put the original value back and let the allocation team know.
