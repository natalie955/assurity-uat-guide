User Acceptance Testing guide

A walk-through of the new system from the first enquiry to the contract renewal, board by board, with the tests we need you to run before go-live.

Prepared by Mutherboard · October 2026 · Version 1

Before you start

This guide explains how each part of the system works and then asks you to try it. You do not need to know monday.com already. Work through the sections in order if you can, because each one picks up the test record from the one before.

What you need

  • Your monday.com login. Accept the invitation email from monday.com if you have not done so yet.
  • Access to the CRM workspace. You will find the boards and apps in the left-hand menu.
  • About 3 to 4 hours for the full run, or 30 to 45 minutes per section.
  • For some tests, a colleague. These are marked with the role you need (for example, a lab user or an allocator).

Test data rules

  • Start every record you create with TEST – and your first name, for example TEST – Sam – Riverside Offices. This keeps test data easy to find and delete.
  • Do not change real client records. You can open and read them, but make changes only on your TEST records.
  • Do not send quotes or emails to real clients. Use your own email address as the client contact.

Recording your results

Under each test, choose Pass, Fail or Blocked (you could not run it), and add a note for anything that is not a pass: what you did, what you expected and what you saw. A link to the item or a screenshot helps us fix things quickly.

Your marks are saved in this browser only. When you finish, go to Send your results, copy them and email them to the Mutherboard project team.

Tests marked “Overnight” depend on a date. monday.com checks dates each morning, so set the date as the test describes and check the result the next day.

How it all fits together

Everything in the system is stored on boards. A board is like a spreadsheet: each row is an item (a lead, a site, a visit) and each column holds one piece of information about it. Boards are linked, so a visit knows its site, its quote and its contract.

On top of the boards sit six apps. An app is a screen that does a multi-step job for you, such as building a quote or allocating a visit, and writes the results back to the boards.

The journey, from enquiry to renewal

  1. Enquiry comes inLogged as a lead, given an owner, then qualified or rejected.Leads board
  2. Deal is openedA qualified lead becomes an opportunity, linked to its account, contacts and sites.Opportunities board
  3. Quote is builtLines per site and work type, with visits, pricing, timings and planned lab samples.Quote Builder app
  4. Client approvesThe client opens a secure link, approves and gives order details. The deal becomes Won.Approval portal
  5. Order is processedThe Quotes & Orders team checks the order and creates every site visit.Order Processor app
  6. Visits are allocatedPlanners match each visit to the right consultant and days.Workload Planner app
  7. Samples are plannedConsultants plan lab samples per day; the lab watches capacity.Sample Planner app
  8. Visit and reportThe visit moves through its stages to a published report, tracked against its deadline.Site Visit Workflow board
  9. Contract renewsSix months before renewal, the contract is copied forward, reviewed and made live, and the cycle starts again at step 5.Contract Renewal Hub app

BoardSales and marketing

D. Quote Builder and the Quotes & Invoices board

AppBoardSales, account managers

A quote is made of lines. Each line is one work type at one site, for example “Legionella Risk Assessment at Site A, quarterly”. Every line is stored as a row on the Quotes & Invoices board, linked to its opportunity and site.

Quotes are always built in the Quote Builder app, which is why the Quotes & Invoices board has no “+ Add item” button. The app works out the number of visits and the totals, keeps pricing consistent and creates the proposal document.

F. Order Processor

AppQuotes & Orders team

When a deal is Won, it appears in the Order Processor’s Orders to process list. The team checks that the order is complete, then submits it. The app creates every site visit for the contract and the sample planning rows, sets the order and report numbers, and tells finance.

  • The deal is Won and has a Confirmation Type; a purchase order also needs its reference.
  • Invoice Address, Invoicing Email and Invoice Frequency are filled in.
  • Every line has its frequency and first visit week number.

Only people in the processing team (set in the app’s Settings) can submit or reject orders.

It ranks consultants by skills and competencies, distance, current workload, targets and availability, and warns when someone would be overloaded. It never blocks a decision: the allocator always has the final say.

I. Sample Planner

AppSamples by Day · Lab CapacityConsultants, lab

The lab can only process a set number of samples each day. The Sample Planner shows consultants how many samples they plan to take on each visit day and how much lab capacity is left, so busy days are spotted early. Going over capacity is never blocked: the consultant gives a reason and the lab approves it or asks for the date to move.

Each visit day is one row on the Samples by Day board. The sample date follows the visit’s planned dates automatically, so you change dates in the Workload Planner, not here. Daily limits live on the Lab Capacity board and are managed in the app’s Admin screen.

K. Contract Renewal Hub

AppAccount managers, CS managers, QC reviewers

Six months before a contract’s 🟠Renewal Date, the owner is reminded to start the renewal. In the Renewal Hub, Copy Forward creates the renewal: a new opportunity linked to the old one, with every active line copied into the next contract period. You then review prices, check the planned work, pass it through quality check, send it to the client and, once accepted, make it live. A live renewal goes back to the Order Processor, so its visits are created like any new order.

Renewal stages

Not Started→In Progress→Pending Quality Check→Send Documents to Customer→Waiting for Renewal→Live

A reviewer can send it back with Returned to Consultant. When the renewal goes live, the old contract is marked Reported.

Help and FAQs

I can’t see “+ Add item” on the Quotes & Invoices board.
That is by design. Quote lines are always created in the Quote Builder so prices and visit numbers stay consistent.
I can’t allocate in the Workload Planner.
Ask an admin to add you under Admin → Settings → Allocators.
I can’t submit an order in the Order Processor.
Check the Checks tab. It lists what is missing, usually an invoicing detail on the opportunity or a first visit week on a line. If every check passes, ask an admin to add you to the processing team in Settings.
A reminder or SLA flag didn’t change.
Date-based automations run once each morning. Check the next day, and make sure the item has an Owner or Assigned Consultant to notify.
Where are invoices raised?
Invoicing stays in Sage Line 50 for now. The order details in monday.com are what finance works from.
Why are some columns marked 🟦, 🟩 or 🟨?
They will be filled by SVR, Assurity Plus and the lab system once those are connected. Until then, fill them in by hand where you need them.
I changed something on a real client by mistake.
Open the item, click the activity log (clock icon) to see the previous value, and change it back. Tell us so we can check.
