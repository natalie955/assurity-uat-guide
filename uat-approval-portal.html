Assurity Consulting · Quote Approval Portal

Quote Approval Portal: in-depth test script

What your clients see when they open a proposal link: reviewing the quote, approving online, approving with a purchase order or a signed page, declining, and what each response changes in monday.com.

Prepared by Mutherboard · November 2026 · Version 1 · 31 tests · About 1½ hours · For: Sales and the Quotes & Orders team, acting as the client

Before you start

The Quote Approval Portal is the page your client opens from the link in a proposal email. They do not need a monday.com login. From the page they can read the quote, download the PDF, approve it (with their invoicing details and, optionally, a purchase order) or decline it. Their answer is written straight onto the quote lines and the opportunity.

In these tests you play the client. Every link must come from a proposal you sent to your own email address.

What you need

  • A monday.com login with access to the Quote Builder, Opportunities, Companies and Quotes & Invoices.
  • Access to your own inbox, plus a phone to check the page on a small screen.
  • A small PDF to use as a purchase order, and a second PDF or photo to use as a signed signature page.

Test data

  • A TEST – yourname company with Signing Preference set to Electronic Approval, and a contact on it whose email is your own.
  • A TEST opportunity for that company with at least one site.
  • Several small proposals built and sent from the Quote Builder (see the Quote Builder script). Each approval or decline uses up its link, so you will need about six. Use a one-line, one-site proposal for most tests and a two-site proposal (one repeat line, one one-off line) for A2.
Never forward a portal link to a real client during testing, and never approve a real client’s quote.

Recording your results

Under each test, choose Pass, Fail or Blocked (you could not run it), and add a note for anything that is not a pass: what you did, what you expected and what you saw. A link to the item or a screenshot helps us fix things quickly.

Your marks are saved in this browser only, separately for each app’s script. When you finish, go to Send your results, copy them and email them to the Mutherboard project team.

Tests marked “Overnight” depend on a date check that runs each morning. Set things up as the test describes and check the result the next day.

A. Opening and reviewing the quote

You, as the client

The link in the proposal email opens a page for that proposal only. It shows the quote number, the company and contact, the sites, the first-year and contract totals, and a button to download the PDF that was sent.

A1

The email link opens the quote

Client
  1. Send a one-line proposal to yourself from the Quote Builder.
  2. Open the email and click the approval link.

You should seeA page headed Quotation with Q and the quote number, the line “Review and approve your Assurity quote.”, your TEST company name, “For the attention of” your contact name, and the site name in a small tag.

A2

Totals match the proposal

Client
  1. Open the link for your two-site proposal (a repeat line and a one-off line).
  2. Compare First year and Contract total with the totals in the Quote Builder.

You should seeBoth sites are listed. First year = one-off lines in full plus one year of each repeat line; Contract total = one-off lines plus each repeat line × its contract years. Example: £250 per visit quarterly for 2 years (£1,000 a year, £2,000 contract) plus a £600 one-off gives First year £1,600 and Contract total £2,600.

A3

Download the PDF

Client
  1. Click Download PDF.

You should seeThe proposal PDF that was sent downloads (its file name ends in -sent.pdf) and matches the document in the Quote Builder.

A4

Read the Terms & Conditions

Client
  1. On the Approve tab, click Read the Terms & Conditions.

You should seeThe proposal PDF opens in a new browser tab, so the client can read the terms section. If your browser blocks pop-ups, allow them for this page and try again.

A5

Works on a phone

Client
  1. Open the same link on your phone.
  2. Switch between Approve and Decline and open both optional sections on the Approve tab.

You should seeEverything fits the screen without sideways scrolling, the buttons are easy to tap and every field can be filled in.

A6

A broken link is refused

Client
  1. Copy the link, change or delete a few characters at the end, and open it.
  2. Open the portal address with nothing after it.

You should seeBoth show “This link is no longer valid” and no quote details.

B. Approving online

You as the client, then you as Sales

For companies whose Signing Preference is Electronic Approval, the client types their name and position, gives invoicing details and ticks the terms box. Approving marks every line on the proposal as Approved, records the order details on the opportunity and tells the opportunity owner.

B1

The app says what is missing

Client
  1. On a fresh link, open the Approve tab and click Approve quote without filling anything in. (If your browser highlights an empty field first, fill in only Full name and Position and click again.)

You should seeA red message beginning “Please complete:” that lists what is still missing, such as the invoice address, a valid invoicing email and ticking the Terms & Conditions box. Nothing is approved.

B2

Invoice details are checked

Client
  1. Fill in Full name and Position and tick the terms box.
  2. Enter an invoice address of only 3 characters and an invoicing email without an @ sign, then click Approve quote.

You should seeThe “Please complete:” message lists the invoice address and a valid invoicing email. Correct both and the message clears on the next attempt.

B3

PO expiry date limits

Client
  1. Open Add a purchase order (optional).
  2. Try to set PO expiry to today, then to a date more than 10 years away.

You should seeThe date picker only allows dates from tomorrow up to 10 years ahead. If an earlier or later date is typed in, the page says “Please complete: a PO expiry date after today and within 10 years.”

B4

Approve without a purchase order

Client
  1. Fill in Full name, Position, Invoice address and Invoicing email, and tick I accept the quote and the Terms & Conditions.
  2. Leave the optional sections closed and click Approve quote.

You should seeA “Thank you” page: “Your approval has been received. Our team will be in touch to arrange the next steps.”

B5

Quote lines are marked Approved

Sales
  1. Open the Quotes & Invoices board and find the lines of the proposal you approved in B4.

You should seeEvery line has Status Approved, Order Confirmation Type Customer Email and Order Date today. The approval link on the line has been cleared.

B6

The opportunity records the order

Sales
  1. Open the TEST opportunity on the Opportunities board.

You should seeOrder Date today, Confirmation Type Customer Email, and the Invoice Address and Invoicing Email you typed in the portal. If this was the only open proposal on the deal, Deal Stage is Won and Closed Date is today.

B7

Update and notification

Sales
  1. Open the opportunity’s Updates.
  2. Check the notifications (bell) of the opportunity owner.

You should seeOne update: “Quote Q… — APPROVED online by [name] ([position]) on [date and time]. Accepted the quote and the Terms & Conditions.” The owner gets a notification “Quote Q… was approved by the client.” with a link to the deal.

B8

The link can only be used once

Client
  1. Open the same approval link again, or refresh the page.

You should see“This link is no longer valid”. The client cannot approve or decline twice.

C. Approving with a purchase order

You as the client, then you as Sales

The client can add a PO number, a PO expiry date and the PO document. The PO is saved on the quote lines and on the opportunity, and the confirmation type becomes Purchase Order Document.

C1

Approve with a full purchase order

Client
  1. On a fresh link, fill in the approval details.
  2. Open Add a purchase order (optional), enter a PO number (e.g. TEST-PO-001), a PO expiry about a year ahead, and attach your PO PDF.
  3. Click Approve quote.

You should seeThe Thank you page.

C2

PO details on the quote lines

Sales
  1. Open the proposal’s lines on Quotes & Invoices.

You should seeStatus Approved, Order Confirmation Type Purchase Order Document, Customer Order Reference TEST-PO-001, PO Expiry the date you chose (dd/mm/yyyy), and a file named Q[quote no]-PO in Quote Proposal Document.

C3

PO details on the opportunity

Sales
  1. Open the TEST opportunity.

You should seeConfirmation Type Purchase Order Document, Customer Order Ref TEST-PO-001, the PO expiry date, and the PO file in PO Document. The update ends with “PO: TEST-PO-001, expiry [date]. PO attached.”

C4

PO number without a document

Client
  1. On a fresh link, approve with a PO number only (no expiry, no file).
  2. Check the lines and the opportunity.

You should seeThe PO number is saved as the Customer Order Reference / Customer Order Ref, and the confirmation type is Customer Email because no PO document was attached.

C5

Optional signed contract with online approval

Client
  1. On a fresh link, fill in the approval details and open Signing with wet ink? Upload your signed contract (optional).
  2. Attach a PDF and approve.

You should seeThe approval goes through. The lines carry a file named Q[quote no]-signed, and the opportunity update says “Signed copy uploaded.”

D. Manual Approval companies

You as Sales, then as the client

When a company’s Signing Preference is Manual Approval, the client must upload a signed signature page instead of typing their name. The proposal document then includes a signature page for them to print and sign.

D1

Switch the company to Manual Approval

Sales
  1. On the Companies board, set your TEST company’s Signing Preference to Manual Approval.
  2. In the Quote Builder, generate and send a new proposal for that company.

You should seeThe proposal PDF ends with a Signature page.

D2

The page asks for a signed page

Client
  1. Open the new link and look at the Approve tab.

You should see“This quote requires manual approval. Please upload your signed signature page before approving.” and a required upload field. There is no Full name, Position or terms tick box, and Approve quote is greyed out until a file is chosen.

D3

Approve with a signed page

Client
  1. Attach your signed page, fill in the invoice address and invoicing email, and click Approve quote.

You should seeThe Thank you page.

D4

Signed page saved and recorded

Sales
  1. Check the proposal’s lines on Quotes & Invoices and the opportunity’s Updates.
  2. Afterwards, set the company’s Signing Preference back to Electronic Approval.

You should seeThe lines are Approved and carry a file named Q[quote no]-signed. The update reads “Quote Q… — APPROVED online via signed signature page on [date]. Signed signature page uploaded.”

E. Declining a quote

You as the client, then you as Sales

The client can decline with a reason. The lines become Declined, the reason is recorded on the opportunity and the owner is told.

E1

A reason is required

Client
  1. On a fresh link, open the Decline tab.
  2. Type one or two characters in Reason for declining.

You should seeDecline quote stays greyed out until the reason has at least 3 characters.

E2

Decline the quote

Client
  1. Enter a reason, e.g. “TEST – budget not approved”, and click Decline quote.

You should seeA Thank you page: “Your response has been received. We appreciate you letting us know.”

E3

Lines, note and notification

Sales
  1. Check the proposal’s lines, then open the opportunity: its Updates, its General Note and the owner’s notifications.

You should seeThe lines have Status Declined. The opportunity has one update “Quote Q… — DECLINED online by the client on [date]. Reason: TEST – budget not approved”, the same sentence is added to the end of General Note (earlier notes are kept), and the owner is notified that the quote was declined. Deal Stage is not changed.

E4

Declined link can’t be reused

Client
  1. Open the declined link again.

You should see“This link is no longer valid”.

F. Versions and deals with several proposals

Sales

A link only works while its lines are still open. Revising a proposal replaces the old lines, so the old link stops working. A deal only becomes Won when none of its quote lines are still open.

F1

An old version’s link stops working

Sales
  1. Send a proposal to yourself and keep the email.
  2. In the Quote Builder, revise that proposal (new version) and send it again.
  3. Open the link in the first email, then the link in the second.

You should seeThe first link shows “This link is no longer valid”. The second opens the new version with its updated totals.

F2

Deal stays open while another proposal is pending

SalesTwo proposals
  1. On one TEST opportunity, send two separate proposals (two quote numbers).
  2. Approve the first in the portal and check the opportunity.
  3. Approve the second and check again.

You should seeAfter the first approval, Deal Stage is unchanged but the order details are recorded. After the second approval, Deal Stage becomes Won with today’s Closed Date.

F4

Declining the last open proposal

SalesTwo proposals
  1. Repeat F2 on another TEST opportunity, but decline the second proposal instead of approving it.

You should seeThe deal is not moved to Won automatically, because a decline never changes the Deal Stage. Record what you see: we need to agree whether the deal should become Won in this case.

F3

Approval works without a monday.com login

Client
  1. Open a fresh link in a private (incognito) browser window where you are not signed in to monday.com.

You should seeThe quote page opens and can be approved or declined without signing in.

Help and FAQs

The client says the link “is no longer valid”.
The proposal has already been approved or declined, or it was replaced by a newer version. Check the lines’ Status on Quotes & Invoices. If the client needs to respond again, send the current version from the Quote Builder.
The page says the quote document isn’t available yet.
The proposal was not sent as a PDF from the Quote Builder. The client can only decline until a PDF is sent. Send the proposal again from the Quote Builder.
The deal didn’t move to Won after the client approved.
Another quote line on the same opportunity is still open. The deal becomes Won when the client approves the last open proposal. If the last one is declined instead, set Deal Stage to Won by hand.
The update mentions a warning that follow-up steps failed.
The approval itself is saved, but something like a file upload did not complete. The warning lists what failed and repeats the client’s invoice details so you can finish it by hand.
A client approved by phone or post.
Use Record approval in the Quote Builder instead of the portal.
