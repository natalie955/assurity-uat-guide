# Assurity monday.com UAT guide

The user acceptance testing guide for Assurity Consulting's new monday.com CRM, prepared by Mutherboard.

**Open the guide:** https://natalie955.github.io/assurity-uat-guide/

**How it all fits together (training overview):** https://natalie955.github.io/assurity-uat-guide/training.html — every board and app, what it is for and how they connect.

## How to use it

1. Open the link above in Chrome, Edge or Safari and sign in to monday.com in another tab.
2. Work through the sections in order. Each one explains part of the system, then lists the tests to run.
3. Under each test, mark **Pass**, **Fail** or **Blocked** and add a note for anything that isn't a pass.
4. When you finish, go to **Send your results**, click **Copy my results** and paste them into an email to the Mutherboard project team.

Your marks are saved in your own browser only. Use the same computer and browser until you send your results.
