monday.com in one minute

  • A board is like a spreadsheet. Each row is an item (a lead, a site, a visit) and each column holds one piece of information about it.
  • Click an item's name to open its item card. It holds every column, plus Updates (the conversation about that item, where you can @mention colleagues), Files and the Activity log (who changed what, and when).
  • Link columns (🔗) connect an item to items on other boards. Click a linked item to jump to it.
  • Apps are screens built on top of the boards. Each one does a multi-step job, such as building a quote or allocating a visit, and writes the results back to the boards.
  • Automations do routine updates for you: set a date, change a status, send a notification. They are listed in What happens automatically.

The journey, from enquiry to renewal

  1. Enquiry comes inLogged as a lead, given an owner, then qualified or rejected.Leads
  2. Deal is openedQualifying a lead creates an opportunity, linked to its company, contacts and sites.Opportunities
  3. Quote is builtOne line per site and work type, with visits, pricing, timings and planned lab samples, then a proposal document.Quote Builder → Quotes & Invoices
  4. Client approvesThe client opens a secure link, approves and gives order details. The deal becomes Won.Quote Approval Portal
  5. Order is processedThe Quotes & Orders team checks the order and creates every site visit and sample day.Order Processor → Visits, Samples by Day
  6. Visits are allocatedPlanners match each visit to the right consultant and days. The report deadline is set.Workload Planner → Visits
  7. Samples are plannedConsultants plan lab samples per day; the lab watches daily capacity.Sample Planner → Samples by Day
  8. Visit and reportThe visit moves through its stages to a published report, tracked against its deadline.Visits
  9. Contract renewsSix months before renewal the contract is copied forward, reviewed and made live, and the cycle starts again at step 5.Contract Renewal Hub

2. Quoting and approval

Quotes are built in the Quote Builder app, stored as lines on the Quotes & Invoices board, and approved by the client in the Quote Approval Portal.

Quote Builder

AppSales and account managers

Builds and sends proposals. Open it from the Vibe Apps folder and pick the opportunity you are quoting.

Opportunity details

  • Company, contacts, owner, Signing Preference
  • Description, close-by date, stage, source
  • Lists any fields still needed before you can quote

Create quote: 6 steps

  1. Recipient
  2. Work types
  3. Frequency (one-off, repeat or contract; visits per year; first visit week)
  4. Sites
  5. Costing: hours, rates, lab and equipment costs, prime cost, margin and the selling price per visit
  6. Review & save: one line per site × work type, all with one Quote No

The proposal

  • Generate the Word document: letter, site schedule, costs, technical specs, T&Cs, order form
  • Edit it if needed, then Upload edited file or mark it reviewed
  • Send: saves the PDF and emails the client a link to view, download and approve it
  • Plan the presentation, Record approval for verbal or posted approvals, Revise to make a new version

Writes to

Quotes & InvoicesOpportunitiesCompaniesSitesActivities

Admin pages hold the standard paragraphs, letter template, tech specs, test methods, email wording and default rates.

Working on it→Generated→Reviewed→Sent to client→ApprovedorDeclined

Quote Approval Portal

Client pageClients (no login)

Lets the client approve or decline a quote online. They open it from the “View and approve your quote” button in the proposal email.

The client sees

  • Quote No, company, “For the attention of”, the sites
  • First-year and contract totals
  • Download PDF and Read the Terms & Conditions

To approve, they give

  • Name and position, and tick to accept the T&Cs, or upload the signed signature page if the company is set to Manual Approval
  • Invoice address and invoicing email
  • Optionally a PO number, expiry date and PO document

Then the system

  • Marks the lines Approved (or Declined, with the reason)
  • Moves the deal to Won and records the order details
  • Posts one update on the deal and notifies the owner

Good to know

  • Each link works once. After a response it shows “This link is no longer valid”
  • Revising a quote cancels the old link; the new version gets its own link when it is sent
Show them

3. Delivering the work

Once a deal is Won, the order is processed into visits, the visits are allocated to consultants, lab samples are planned, and each visit moves through to a published report.

Order Processor

AppQuotes & Orders team

Turns a won order into visits. Every Won deal appears in Orders to process.

Before submitting

  • Check Order details, Lines and Checks
  • Checks include: confirmation type and PO reference, invoice address, email and frequency, and a frequency and first visit week on every line

Submit for processing

  • Creates every visit for the contract on Visits, at Ready for Allocation
  • Creates the sample day rows on Samples by Day
  • Sets the order and report numbers, marks the deal Processed, and notifies the owner and finance

Or reject

  • Reject order with a note: the owner and CS manager are told what to fix

Good to know

  • Only people in the processing team (set in the app's Settings) can submit or reject
  • Processed orders move to History

Sample Planner

AppConsultants and the lab

Plans lab samples per visit day against the lab's daily capacity, so busy days are spotted early. Going over capacity is never blocked: the consultant gives a reason and the lab responds.

Screens

  • My samples, Team samples
  • Lab view: day by day capacity and requests
  • Reports, Admin

How it works

  • Planned samples come from the quote
  • Sample dates follow the visit: change dates in the Workload Planner
  • Capacity shows green, amber near the limit, red over it

Over capacity

  • The consultant gives a reason: Capacity Request becomes Requested and the lab is notified
  • The lab approves, or asks for the date to move

5. Renewing contracts

Contract Renewal Hub

AppAccount and CS managers, QC reviewers

Renews a contract. Six months before a contract's 🟠Renewal Date the owner is reminded to start. The renewal is a new opportunity linked to the old one.

Steps

  1. Renewal urgency or Choose a contract
  2. Copy Forward: creates the renewal opportunity with every active line in the new period
  3. Apply a Price Change (with Undo), add or remove lines, run the anomaly check
  4. Submit for quality check: the reviewer passes it or returns it with feedback
  5. Send proposal to customer
  6. Make Contract Live: record the acceptance and PO

Make Contract Live

  • Renewal becomes Won and Live, and goes to the Order Processor (Awaiting Processing)
  • The old contract is marked Reported
  • The people set in the app's Settings are notified

Uses

OpportunitiesQuotes & Invoices

Columns marked 🟠 belong to this app.

Not Started→In Progress→Pending Quality Check→Send Documents to Customer→Waiting for Renewal→Live

Everyday tips

Find anything
Use the search at the top of monday.com, or the search box on a board. On a board, the Person filter shows only your items.
Talk about an item
Open the item card and write in Updates. Type @ and a name to notify a colleague.
Undo a mistake
Open the item card's Activity log to see the previous value, and change it back.
Notifications
The bell at the top shows anything assigned to you, reminders and @mentions.
On the move
The monday.com mobile app shows the same boards with the same login, so consultants can check and update visits on site.
Practise safely
Start any practice record with TEST – and your name, and never send quotes to real clients while practising.
