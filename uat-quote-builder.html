Quote Builder: in-depth test script

Every part of the Quote Builder: finding opportunities, building, costing and saving proposals, what lands on the boards, editing, revising and approving quotes, the quotes dashboard, proposal documents, sending, and the admin screens.

Prepared by Mutherboard · November 2026 · Version 1 · 113 tests · 4–5 hours · For: Sales and account managers, the Quotes and Orders team, and a Content admin for the admin tests

Before you start

The Quote Builder is where every quote for Assurity Consulting is built. You pick an opportunity, build a proposal made of lines (one line = one work type at one site), cost and price each line, and save it. Each line becomes a row on the Quotes & Invoices board, linked to the opportunity, the site and the contact. From there the proposal document is produced, sent to the client and, once approved, the deal is marked Won.

What you need

  • A monday.com login that can open the Quote Builder app and the Opportunities, Companies, People, Sites and Quotes & Invoices boards.
  • Your own email address. All test contacts use it, so nothing is ever sent to a client.
  • A Content admin you can call on (for B5, and to tell you how the settings below are set). Only Content admins see the Admin button.
  • Settings an admin must have in place (Admin → Settings unless shown): Default hourly rates; Fire tier default hours (per visit) filled for at least one tier, with at least one tier left at 0 if possible; the quote validity period (30 days unless changed); Required fields to quote left at the default (Source, Account, Contact, Owner); at least one “Always include” paragraph in Standard Paragraphs.
  • The second part of this script (proposal documents, review and sending). Several tests in section F need a proposal that has been generated, reviewed or sent. Send only to your own email address.
  • A small PDF file to use as a pretend purchase order.

Test data

  • Replace “Sam” with your own first name everywhere below.
  • Companies board: add TEST – Sam – Riverside Offices Ltd and TEST – Sam – Other Company Ltd.
  • People board: add TEST – Sam – Main Contact and TEST – Sam – Second Contact. Give both your own email address and link both to TEST – Sam – Riverside Offices Ltd in their 🔗Account column.
  • Opportunities board: add TEST – Sam – Quote Builder with only the Owner set (you). Leave 🔗Account, Contacts, Source, Expected Close Date and Deal Value empty – section B fills them in through the app.
  • Opportunities board: add TEST – Sam – Other Deal with Owner = you, 🔗Account = TEST – Sam – Other Company Ltd, Contacts = TEST – Sam – Main Contact and any Source.
  • Sites are created during the tests: TEST – Sam – Warehouse (C1), TEST – Sam – Site A and TEST – Sam – Site B (C9).
  • Write down every Quote No and Site Code the app gives you – later tests refer back to them.

Recording your results

Under each test, choose Pass, Fail or Blocked (you could not run it), and add a note for anything that is not a pass: what you did, what you expected and what you saw. A link to the item or a screenshot helps us fix things quickly.

Your marks are saved in this browser only, separately for each app’s script. When you finish, go to Send your results, copy them and email them to the Mutherboard project team.

Tests marked “Overnight” depend on a date check that runs each morning. Set things up as the test describes and check the result the next day.

D. Cost and price each line

Sales, account managers

Step 5 shows one costing card per site and work type. Click a card’s heading to open it. Each card has its own frequency, time grid, rates, other costs and a selling price. The selling price is always the price per visit; the annual and contract values are worked out from it. You cannot move on until every card has a selling price.

How the numbers are worked out. Prime cost per visit = (consultant hours ÷ number of visit columns) × consultant rate + (support hours ÷ number of visit columns) × support rate + lab and asbestos sample costs + equipment, external analysis and courier costs. Annual = per visit × visits per year (one-off: × Number of Visits). Contract = annual × Contract Period (one-off: same as annual). Margin = selling price − prime cost. Margin % = margin ÷ selling price.
The worked examples use a consultant rate of £75 and a support rate of £25 – type these into each card if the pre-filled rates differ. They also assume the Standard support cost row shows 0. If it shows a number, add that number × £25 to every prime cost. Once you start costing, do not change anything in step 3 – it would overwrite the frequency on every card for that work type.

Help and FAQs

Why is “Create quote” greyed out?
A field your admin has made required is empty on the opportunity. The red box under the button says which one (“Add … before creating a quote”). Fill it in there and it is saved to the opportunity. Admins choose these fields in Admin → Required fields to quote.
Why can’t I edit or regenerate my proposal?
It has been sent or approved, so it is locked (“This proposal has been sent. Use Revise to make changes.”). Click Revise and give a reason. This creates a new version (v2, v3 …) that you can edit, and the old version moves to History as read only.
What does “Superseded” mean?
An older version that has been replaced by a revision. It can’t be changed, its values are not counted in totals, and its approval link no longer works.
Why is Send greyed out?
The proposal must be generated and then reviewed. Either tick Reviewed — no edits needed or click Upload edited file. The text next to Send tells you which step is missing.
I changed a price after reviewing. Why is it back to Generated?
On purpose: any change to a line, and any regeneration, means the document must be checked again. Regenerate, review and then send.
Where is the Word file saved?
In the Quote Proposal Document column of every line of the proposal on the Quotes & Invoices board. Drafts are named Q(Quote No)-v(version)-draft.docx, edited uploads -edited.docx and the sent PDF -sent.pdf. The card’s “Current document” link opens the latest one.
Who signs the letter, and why is there a blank space instead of a signature?
The Assigned Consultant on the lines (copied from the opportunity Owner when the quote was created) signs the letter, or you if none is set. If that person hasn’t uploaded a signature image on the Signature page, the letter leaves a blank space for a handwritten signature.
Why does the Ref only show the Quote No?
The full Ref (consultant initials/your initials/Quote No) needs a real Assigned Consultant. Set one on the lines and regenerate.
The message says “no technical specification set for …”. What do I do?
That work type has no saved specification, so none is included. Ask a Content admin to add it in Admin → Technical specifications, then regenerate.
Why does the letter say “Dear Sir/Madam”?
The contact has no First Name and no name on the People board. Fill in First Name and regenerate.
Why can’t I choose how a site is invoiced?
Sites with only one-off work are always invoiced after each visit. Repeat work can be invoiced after each visit, monthly, quarterly, six-monthly or annually. After changing it, regenerate the document so the wording updates.
The send window says the PDF is over 5 MB.
The PDF can’t be emailed. Remove large added library PDFs, or upload a smaller edited Word file (for example with compressed pictures), then try again.
The email failed. Was anything sent?
No. When the mailbox fails, the window shows Not sent with the reason. The approval link is cleared and the status is unchanged. Click Try again, or try the other mailbox (Gmail or Outlook). If it keeps failing, ask a Content admin to send a test from Admin → Quote email.
The client says the link has expired.
A newer version of the proposal exists (Revise), or the link was replaced. Send the latest version; its email carries a new link.
Can I send several proposals in one email?
Yes, if they are all Reviewed and for the same contact. Tick the boxes in front of the Quote Nos and click Send (n) together. Each proposal gets its own button. You can’t edit the subject or message in that window.
What does “Remember for (company)” do?
It saves your choices in the “What goes into this document” window (parts left out, library replacements and added documents) for that company. The next proposal for the company opens with them already set.
Who can see the Admin menu?
Only Content admins. They are listed, added and removed on Admin → Standard Paragraphs. Everyone can manage their own signature with the Signature button.
How is the Quote No chosen?
It is the higher of the starting number in Admin → Settings and the highest Quote No on the board, plus 1. Numbers already used are skipped.
