Order Processor: in-depth test script

Checking won orders in the Order Processor: the order checks, how every visit is planned, processing (visits, sample rows, Order and Report numbers, notifications), rejecting and resubmitting, interrupted runs, permissions and Settings, and renewals coming back from the Contract Renewal Hub.

Prepared by Mutherboard · November 2026 · Version 1 · 35 tests · 3–4 hours · For: The Quotes & Orders team, with an admin and a Sales colleague for some tests

Before you start

The Order Processor is where the Quotes & Orders team turns a won deal into work. It lists won orders, checks that the order and every quote line are complete, and works out every visit for the whole contract. When you process an order it creates those visits on the Visits board at Ready for Allocation, adds sample planning rows, gives each line a new Order No and Report No, and tells the owner and finance. Orders that are not ready can be sent back to the owner with a reason. The app’s top bar reads Process Order.

What you need

  • You on the Quotes & Orders team list in the app’s Settings. Ask an admin to add you.
  • An admin for Section E and for a few steps in G and H. Admins are the people on the Admins list in Settings. While that list is empty, the Opportunities board owners are the admins.
  • A Sales colleague who is not on the Quotes & Orders team and is not an admin. They will be the Owner and CS Manager on your TEST orders, check notifications, and run some steps themselves.
  • A colleague on the Finance recipients list in Settings to check the finance notification. The admin adds them in E3.
  • Access to the Opportunities, Quotes & Invoices, Visits and Samples by Day boards and the Quote Builder. For Section I you also need the Contract Renewal Hub.
  • A second browser tab for a few tests, and a way to switch your Wi-Fi off for a moment (H1).

D. How visits are planned

Quotes & Orders team

Before anything is created, the app works out how many visits each line has and which week each visit is planned for. To see the plan, click the arrow at the start of a line. Each preview chip reads like “#2 · wk 23 2027 · Yr 1 · £250.00”: the visit number, the planned week and year, the contract year, and the visit value.

How many visits: a one-off line has its quantity, or 1 if there is no quantity. For other lines, the app uses the line’s Number of Visits if it is filled in. Otherwise it multiplies visits per year by Contract Period (Years), rounding 2-yearly lines up.
Visits per year: if Frequency Unit and Frequency Measure are both set, they decide it. Every N weeks = 52 ÷ N, every N months = 12 ÷ N, every N years = 1 ÷ N. Otherwise the app uses Visit Frequency: Quarterly 4, 4-Monthly 3, 6-Monthly 2, Yearly 1, 2-Yearly ½.
Spacing: visits are 52 ÷ (visits per year) weeks apart, rounded. Each new contract year starts again at the first week. A week past 52 carries on from week 1 of the next year. 2-yearly visits are every other year, in the same week. One-off visits all share the first week.
Value: every visit is worth the line’s price per visit.

The team can send an order back with a reason. The order becomes Rejected and stays in the list with a red label. The owner fixes the problem on the boards and clicks Submit for processing. Anyone can submit, and everyone on the Quotes & Orders team list is notified.

H. Interrupted runs and re-processing

Quotes & Orders team

Big orders are created a batch at a time; Batch size in Settings sets how many visits go in each batch. If a run stops part-way, everything created so far is kept and the order shows Continue processing, which finishes the rest without repeating a visit or using new numbers. These tests use Order 3 (38 visits), with Batch size set to 2 so the run is slow enough to interrupt.

Never start or continue the same order in two tabs at once. Wait until one run has stopped before pressing Retry or Continue processing.
H1

Connection lost: Retry

AdminQuotes & Orders team
  1. Admin: set Batch size to 2, save, and write down Next Order No and Next Report No.
  2. Open Order 3. The bar should read “Ready: 4 lines, 38 visits”. Click Process order, then Yes, process order.
  3. When the text reaches about “Creating visit 7 of 38…”, turn your Wi-Fi off.
  4. Turn Wi-Fi back on, wait 30 seconds, then click Retry.
  5. Don’t let it finish: go straight on to H2.

You should seeStep 2: the count goes up about two at a time, with “Large orders can take a while. If you leave, press “Continue processing” later to finish.”
Step 3: a red error message appears in the box (its wording depends on your browser) and the button changes to Retry.
Step 4: the count carries on from where it had reached. It does not start again at 1.

I. Renewals from the Contract Renewal Hub

Account manager, Quotes & Orders team

When a renewal is made live in the Contract Renewal Hub, its Deal Stage becomes Won. Like any won deal, it then appears in Orders to process as Awaiting Processing. The renewal is a new opportunity. Its lines are copies of the previous contract’s lines, with the first-visit year moved on by the contract length and no Order No, Report No or visits yet. Processing it creates the next contract’s visits.

Help and FAQs

My won deal isn’t in Orders to process.
The list only shows orders whose Process Order Status is Awaiting Processing or Rejected, so check Deal Stage and Process Order Status on the Opportunities board. Processed orders leave the list. Search only matches the order name. The list shows 25 at a time: click Load more for older orders.
Why is Process order greyed out?
The text next to it says why, and so does the tip when you hover over it. The reason is one of: Fix the “Must fix” items first, Tick “I’ve checked these” first, Confirm CS Manager sign-off first or No lines left to process.
I can’t edit anything on the order.
Only the Quotes & Orders team and admins can edit. Nobody can edit once the order is processed, or while a processing run is unfinished.
The Lines number in the list doesn’t match the order page.
The list counts every quote linked in 🔗Related Quotes. The order page hides lines that are Cancelled, Declined or Superseded, and renewal lines that were made dead.
Processing stopped part-way. What now?
Open the order and click Continue processing, or Retry if the box is still open. Visits already created are kept and not duplicated, and the lines keep the numbers they were given. Don’t run the same order from two tabs at once.
How are Order No and Report No chosen?
Every line gets its own Order No and Report No, taken from Next Order No and Next Report No in Settings, which then move forward by the number of lines. If those fields are blank, the app starts from the highest number on Quotes & Invoices plus 1.
Where are the visits?
On the Visits board, at Visit Stage Ready for Allocation. The View in Site Visit Workflow button uses the board’s old name. Each visit is named Report No/visit number.
Why do visits 5 onwards have the same samples as visit 4?
The app reads the Visit 1 to Visit 4 sample numbers saved by the Quote Builder, and any later visit uses the Visit 4 numbers. Only day 1 of a visit carries samples. Extra days get empty rows.
My tick boxes have cleared.
“I’ve checked these” and the CS Manager sign-off are not saved. They clear whenever the page reloads, so tick them again just before you process.
Who is told what?
Submitting notifies everyone on the Settings Quotes & Orders team list. Admins who are not on that list are not told.
Rejecting notifies the owner and the CS Manager.
Processing notifies the owner and the Finance recipients, and posts an update on the opportunity.
Can I undo processing?
Not in the app. For TEST orders, ask an admin to delete the TEST visits and sample rows. Never process a real client’s order while testing.
