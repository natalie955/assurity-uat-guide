Assurity Consulting · Sample Planner

Sample Planner: in-depth test script

Every part of the Sample Planner: roles and settings, lab capacity rules, how sample day rows are created and kept in step with visits, planning samples, going over capacity, the team view, Lab view, capacity requests and reports.

Prepared by Mutherboard · November 2026 · Version 1 · 30 tests · 3–4 hours · For: An admin or lab admin, a consultant, a team manager and a lab user (some tests need two people)

Before you start

The Sample Planner lets consultants plan how many lab samples they will take on each day of a site visit, and lets the lab see how full each day is for each sample category. It uses two boards: Samples by Day (one row per visit day, with the number of each lab test) and Lab Capacity (the daily limits and warning levels). Every screen uses the same capacity sums, so the consultant, the manager and the lab all see the same numbers.

What you need

  • Access to the Sample Planner app in monday.com, and to the Samples by Day, Lab Capacity, Visits, Quotes & Invoices and Opportunities boards so you can check what the app writes.
  • An admin set up as in test A1. Do section A first. Until someone is saved in Admins, only the owners of the Samples by Day board can open Admin.
  • Your name on the Consultant Skills & Availability board with a Team filled in. Team samples uses this to work out who is in which team.
  • A colleague in the same team for tests marked Two people, and one person saved as a Lab user (test A1).
  • Access to the Quote Builder, Order Processor and Workload Planner (or someone who can run them for you), and edit rights on the Visits board.

Test data

  • Your test dates. Pick a Thursday 8–11 weeks from today, in a week with no bank holiday. We call it your test Thursday. The next day is your test Friday, then the Monday after, then test Tuesday (the day after that Monday). Open Lab view, set From and to to your test Thursday and check the Leg row shows Planned 0. If it shows another number, add that number to every Leg limit used in tests B2 and D5 (for example, if Planned shows 3, use Max per day 13 instead of 10).
  • A TEST company and site, e.g. “TEST – Sam – Riverside Offices”, with your own email address as the contact.
  • A TEST opportunity, e.g. “TEST – Sam – Sample Planner”, with two quote lines at that site, built in the Quote Builder:
    • Line 1: work type Water Quality Assessment, a frequency with at least 2 visits, 2 days per visit. In Planned work (air / water) enter W1-3 (S) LEG: Visit 1 = 6, Visit 2 = 4, and W8-2 (No PS): Visit 1 = 2, Visit 2 = 2.
    • Line 2: work type Legionella Risk Assessment, 1 day per visit, nothing in Planned work.
    Send it to your own email and approve it in the portal. Do not process it yet – test C1 does that.
  • For test F4 only: a second TEST opportunity, e.g. “TEST – Sam – Renewal samples”, with Opportunity Type = Renewal. It must not be processed.
  • When the order is processed, write down the report number. The visits are named “report number/visit number”, e.g. 12345/1. In this script we call them visit /1, visit /2 and the line 2 visit.

Recording your results

Under each test, choose Pass, Fail or Blocked (you could not run it), and add a note for anything that is not a pass: what you did, what you expected and what you saw. A link to the item or a screenshot helps us fix things quickly.

Your marks are saved in this browser only, separately for each app’s script. When you finish, go to Send your results, copy them and email them to the Mutherboard project team.

Tests marked “Overnight” depend on a date check that runs each morning. Set things up as the test describes and check the result the next day.

B. Lab capacity rules

Admin or Lab admin

Each rule is one item on the Lab Capacity board. For every day and every category, the app picks one limit (Max per day) and one warning level (Warn when this many left). Rules with no categories chosen apply to all six categories. A rule with Max per day left blank never sets the limit. It only adds a cut-off time or message.

Rule typeUsed whenBeats
Date overridesThe day falls between From and To, Max per day is filled in and the rule is ActiveWeekday rules and baselines
Weekday rulesThe day is one of the chosen weekdays and Max per day is filled inBaselines
Baseline limitsEvery day, if nothing above applies—
AnnouncementsNever set a limit. They only show a message on the chosen dates—
Rules apply to everyone as soon as you save, including real consultants planning real visits. Only use your TEST dates, start every rule name with “TEST –” and your first name, and archive your TEST rules when you have finished (see Help).

C. Sample day rows and keeping them in step

Quotes & Orders team, allocator, consultant

Each visit that takes samples has one Samples by Day row per day at site. Day 1's Sample Date is the visit's Planned Start Date. Each later day is the next working day: weekends, England and Wales bank holidays and days the lab is closed are skipped. You can't type a sample date in the app. You move the visit instead.

What changesWhereWhen the sample rows follow
Order processed, quote line has planned samplesOrder ProcessorStraight away: one row per day, day 1 holds the samples, no date or consultant yet
Visit allocated or re-allocatedWorkload PlannerStraight away: Sample Date and Consultant are set
Planned Start Date or Planned Days at SiteVisits boardThe next time someone opens My samples or Team samples showing that visit, Lab view with those dates in range, or Reports → Free capacity for that month
Assigned ConsultantVisits boardThe next time My samples or Team samples showing that visit is opened (Lab view doesn't update the consultant)
A visit with a sample work type but no rows yet—Rows are created the next time its consultant opens My samples (or a manager opens Team samples)
The app does not update rows on a timer. To make it catch up after you change a visit on the Visits board, open Lab view with the new date (or the old one) in the range, or reload My samples. Lab view and Free capacity then show Updated 1 visit's sample dates (or Updated N visits' sample dates). If a lot needs updating, the page stops after about 10–12 seconds. My samples may then say N visits are still being set up – refresh in a moment to see them. Reload to finish.

Help and FAQs

I changed a visit on the Visits board but the sample dates haven't moved. How long do I wait?
There is no timer. The rows catch up when someone opens My samples or Team samples showing that visit, Lab view with the date in range, or Reports → Free capacity for that month. Open one of those pages or reload it. Allocating or moving a visit in the Workload Planner updates the rows straight away.
Why can't I type a sample date?
Sample dates come from the visit's Planned Start Date and Planned Days at Site. Day 1 is the start date and each next day is the next working day, skipping weekends, England and Wales bank holidays and lab-closed days. Move the visit instead.
Why do some rows show 0 in every box and others are blank?
Rows made by the Order Processor are filled with 0. Rows the Sample Planner makes itself are left blank. Both count as nothing planned.
Where do the starting numbers come from?
From 🟢Planned Samples (per visit) on the quote line, entered in the Quote Builder's Planned work (air / water) grid. Visit 1 uses the Visit 1 column, and so on. Visits 5 and later use the Visit 4 numbers. Only Day 1 gets them.
Will the app stop me going over capacity?
No. Your number is always saved. If it takes the day to 0 or below for that category, you are asked for a reason so the lab can be told. Lowering a number never shows the warning.
Who hears about a capacity request?
Everyone in Lab users gets a monday.com notification, and an update is posted on the visit. No email is sent. The lab inbox address in the pop-up is only a link you can use yourself.
Why are the boxes greyed out on some rows?
You can only edit visits where you are the Assigned Consultant, visits of consultants in the team you manage, or any visit if you are an admin or a manager for All teams.
The limit isn't what I expected.
A Date override beats a Weekday rule, which beats the Baseline. Rules that are switched off, or have Max per day left blank, don't set a limit. If two rules of the same type match, the first one on the Lab Capacity board is used.
A page says “still being set up” or “Updated N visits' sample dates”.
The app brought sample dates into line with their visits before showing the numbers. If it ran out of time, reload after a few seconds.
How do I tidy up when I've finished?
In Admin → Capacity rules, archive every TEST rule (Leg limit, early courier, lab notice and any others still active). Put TEST visits back to the stage they started at. Leave TEST opportunities and visits for the admin to remove, as described in the main UAT guide. Remove any Settings entries that were only for testing.
Something didn't load.
My samples shows We couldn't load the samples. with a Try again button. Lab view shows Lab capacity could not be loaded. Reports show Free capacity could not be loaded. or The change log could not be loaded. Take a screenshot, note the time, and log it as a UAT ticket.
